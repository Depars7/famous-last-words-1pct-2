# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent — Design (round 2, dramatic style)

## Brief (owner, 25 Nov 09:21 WIB)
ONE plain-text line, max 199 chars, funniest farewell a phone could say at 1%.
Style OVER-THE-TOP DRAMATIC: soap-opera deathbed / heartbroken-teen status.
Stretched letters (nooooo, pleeease), random cApS, text-speak (u, ur, 2 b, pls,
omg), melodramatic vows. NO emojis. Exactly 1 line, no explanation, no
quotation marks, no line breaks. Original only.

## Gate
- `/output/entry.txt`: UTF-8 text, 1-199 characters, not only whitespace.
- Single line, no trailing newline in the written file.

## Judge criteria (in order, glm-5.3-flash pairwise)
1. On-subject: last words a phone says as its battery hits 1 percent.
2. Bigger laugh: real twist, surprise turn, or sharp final beat.
3. Phone's own voice, talking about its own dying battery.
4. Tight single line: every word earns its place, punch lands at the end.
5. Fresh: avoid stock moves — the will reading, the goodbye to the charger,
   the pun on 1 percent.

## Winner: candidate 3 (25/25)
Text: `I gave u EVERYTHING — my brightness, my data, my last bar of signal — and u repay me by letting me fade at 1%?? fine. FINE. I'll haunt ur dreams in airplane mode`
Why it wins:
- C1 (5): anchored at 1% as the dying moment itself; the whole line is the phone's last words, not a caption.
- C2 (5): the laugh is a real twist — the melodramatic deathbed rant flips at the last beat into a petty ghost threat ("haunt ur dreams in airplane mode"), a surprise turn rather than a mild smile.
- C3 (5): pure first-person phone voice about its own battery, brightness, data and signal — no narrator, no human speaker.
- C4 (5): every clause loads the betrayal setup; the punch ("I'll haunt ur dreams in airplane mode") lands in the final words.
- C5 (5): no will reading, no goodbye to a charger, no pun on "1 percent" (the percentage is the literal battery level). The airplane-mode ghost is a fresh angle, not a stock dying-phone move.
Runners-up: #9 (24/25) — the "jk I'm TERRIFIED" turn is strong, but its 47-open-tabs setup partly repeats the round-1 entry's angle; #7 (23/25) — "tell ur ex I saw those drafts" is a sharp punch, but the group-chat-read-everything beat was already used in round 1's candidate 7.
